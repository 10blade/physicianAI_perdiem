How this list is made

Every morning an automated search reads the public job boards of about 50 AI labs, AI-training platforms and health-AI companies, plus a few careers pages. It keeps roles for physicians (MD/DO or a named specialty) with contract, part-time, per diem or hourly terms, and lists those whose posted pay reaches $100/hr.

Roles posted once per city, or under many specialty names, are merged into one row with a listing count.

Before you apply

  • Most roles are independent-contractor (1099) work: no benefits, and you handle your own taxes.
  • Confirm the hourly rate in writing before any unpaid onboarding. Per-task and "up to" rates depend on speed and tier.
  • Check licensure. Many roles need an active US license; a few accept residents.
  • Clinical roles that involve patient care usually need malpractice cover. Ask who provides it.
