# Physician AI tasks paying $100+/hr: researched snapshot

**Compiled 4 November 2026** from web-search results. The job pages could not be opened from the research environment, so every listing is a lead to confirm. Some may already be closed. Pay is quoted as advertised. For a list that refreshes every morning, see [results/latest.md](results/latest.md).

**How sure each row is:**
- **Own posting**: the platform's own job page.
- **Repost**: a job board's copy of that posting (VC portfolio boards, Remotive, remote.co, …).
- **Aggregator**: a third-party tracker such as remowork.life, aitrainer.work or JobLeads. Confirm these on the platform before applying.
- A **~** in front of a date means it was estimated from when the aggregator first listed the job, not the employer's own post date.
- **undated** means the listing came up in search on 4 Nov 2026 but showed no date.

## Before you apply

- Almost all of these are independent-contractor (1099) roles. There are no benefits and you handle your own taxes.
- Clinical roles, where you see patients (for example Counsel Health), usually need malpractice cover. Ask who provides it.
- Many roles want an active US license. A few accept residents (Turing) or trainees at a lower rate (Glass Health).
- "Up to" and per-task rates depend on speed, tier and project. Get the hourly rate in writing before unpaid onboarding.
